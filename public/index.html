How it works

One algorithm. One decision per day.

No discretion, no second-guessing, no day-trading. The strategy makes ~25 position changes per year on average — most days it tells you to hold.

01

After market close

Each weekday at 5:30 PM ET, the algorithm pulls QQQ and TLT prices from Tiingo. It computes a v3.5.1 signal using validated rules on volume confirmation, a long-term trend filter regime, and a short-term trend filter trend filters.

02

Daily signal

If the signal fires, you receive an SMS and email after the close: BUY QQQ, SELL QQQ to TLT, or SELL QQQ to cash. The signal is the same for every subscriber — no personalization.

03

Execute at next open

Place the trade in your IRA brokerage account at the next market open. The full position rotates — no partial allocations, no leverage, no options. Cash settles same day in an IRA.

Built for tax-advantaged accounts

Why this strategy is ideal for IRAs

Zero tax drag

The strategy fires ~25 trades per year. In a taxable account, every gain is short-term and taxed at ordinary income rates — that compound friction destroys the strategy's edge. In an IRA, rotation is tax-free until withdrawal.

Same-day cash settlement

When you sell QQQ in an IRA, the proceeds are available immediately for the next purchase — no T+1 wait, no good-faith violation, no settled-funds rules.

Two-fund simplicity

Only QQQ, TLT, and cash. Both funds are highly liquid, low-fee ETFs available in every major IRA custodian (Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, etc.) without commissions.
