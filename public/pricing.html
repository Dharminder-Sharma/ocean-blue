Why so cheap?

Because the algorithm is automated. There’s no team of analysts to pay, no research department, no high-touch support overhead. The signal runs once a day after close, gets transmitted to every subscriber simultaneously, and the operation costs scale with subscribers but not effort.

At $20/month, the service pays for itself with as little as one well-timed rotation per year. Most subscribers will save dramatically more than the annual fee in any single bear market we navigate.
