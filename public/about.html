About

One algorithm. Built in Brooklyn.

Ocean Blue is the signal service from Best Kind LLC — an independent inventor and research firm based in Brooklyn, NY. This page tells you who we are, how we got here, and why we think a rules-based rotation strategy deserves a place in self-directed retirement portfolios.

Why this exists

Most retail investors hold an IRA, buy index funds, and ride them through every drawdown. That works — it has produced extraordinary returns over the long term for the disciplined. But it also means watching half your retirement savings vanish in 2008 or 2022, with no plan but "hold and hope."

For investors with multi-decade horizons, that approach is mathematically optimal even if emotionally brutal. For investors within 10-15 years of needing the money, the sequence of returns starts to matter as much as the long-run average. A 50% drawdown five years before retirement is a different problem than the same drawdown 25 years out.

Ocean Blue exists because there's a defensible middle path between "100% in QQQ, hope for the best" and "60/40 balanced fund, settle for 8% returns." The strategy stays in equities when the regime supports them, and steps aside — to long Treasuries, or to cash — when the regime breaks.

Who runs Ocean Blue

Best Kind LLC is a Brooklyn-based research firm operated by Ted Bochi, an independent inventor with an active patent portfolio spanning multiple technology domains — wheelchair suspension systems, tamper-evident seals, thermal management for data centers, pest exclusion engineering, and PEMF wellness devices.

Ocean Blue is the latest project: applying the same rigorous engineering approach we use for physical patents to a problem in personal finance. The strategy underwent six-plus months of full-time development. It was tested against 22 years of daily market data, walk-forward validated on out-of-sample periods, and parameter-tested for sensitivity to small variations in inputs.

What we won't do

We won't hide poor performance. Every signal we issue is published. Every paper-trading rotation is in the public record. If we have a bad month, you'll see it on the live tracker the same day we do.

We won't sell your information. Your email and (if you opt in) your phone number exist for the sole purpose of delivering signals. We don't share contact information with any third party.

We won't tinker with the algorithm to chase recent performance. The rules are locked. If they need to change — because market structure has shifted or the strategy has clearly stopped working — we'll publish what changed and why, with the data to back it up.

We won't oversell the strategy. Backtests don't guarantee future results. The strategy can fail. Bull markets can run for years without giving rotation a chance to add value. We've made these caveats prominent throughout the site for a reason.

The 6-month paper trading commitment

Before charging anyone a dollar, we run the algorithm for six months in public paper trading. Every signal goes live on the tracker page in real time. Every daily mark-to-market is visible. If the strategy fails during paper trading, anyone considering subscribing can see that happen before they pay anything.

If the paper trading goes well, subscriptions open. If it doesn't, we won't launch a commercial service.

The pricing logic

$20/month with an optional $5 SMS add-on isn't an accident. It's deliberately low because the algorithm runs automatically and we're not trying to monetize complexity. Compare to most signal services at $500-2,000/year. Most of that pricing reflects marketing overhead and the implicit promise of "alpha" we're not promising.

At $20/month, the service pays for itself even if the strategy only outperforms buy-and-hold by a small margin over a typical year. In a year with a meaningful drawdown that the algorithm catches, it pays for many years of subscription cost in one rotation.
