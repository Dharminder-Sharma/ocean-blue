What we promise on uptime

The signal runs once a day on a single machine, reads from a single market data provider, and publishes to a single static website. That sounds fragile, but it's actually a feature — fewer moving parts, fewer things to go wrong.

If anything in the chain fails, the live tracker will display a yellow "stale data" banner until it catches up, and you'll receive any delayed signals as soon as the system recovers. We don't trade on your behalf, so a delay won't trigger a missed market action — it just means your manual rotation may execute a day later than ideal.

If you've been a subscriber for at least a month, you can email us to ask any question about a particular signal date. Every signal we've ever issued is in the historical record, on this site, publicly. There's no edit history because there are no edits.
