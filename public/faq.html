Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

When will subscriptions open?

After the 6-month public paper trading phase concludes and the regulatory review is finalized. Estimated 6-9 months from launch.

What happens during the paper trading phase?

The algorithm runs daily against live market data. Every signal, every rotation, every daily portfolio value is published in real time on the live tracker page. No money changes hands — this is a public demonstration of how the strategy operates with current market conditions.

How much capital do I need?

Technically, just enough to buy one share of QQQ (currently ~$700) plus one share of TLT (~$85). Practically, the strategy makes most sense with $10,000 or more so the $20/month fee is reasonable relative to expected returns. There is no minimum imposed by Ocean Blue.

Can I use this in a 401(k)?

Only if your 401(k) allows you to buy individual ETFs like QQQ and TLT. Most employer 401(k) plans restrict you to a menu of mutual funds — those won't work. Self-directed 401(k)s and brokerage windows within 401(k)s typically do.

What if I miss a signal?

If you can't execute at the next market open, execute as soon as practical. The tracker shows the official paper trading entry at the next-day open price. Late execution will deviate from the tracker performance — that's part of subscriber-level slippage.

What if I disagree with a signal?

That's entirely your choice — the service publishes signals; you decide whether to act. Many subscribers will inevitably skip signals during periods of strong conviction. Just understand that doing so changes the strategy's risk profile and may eliminate the historical edge.

Why QQQ specifically? Not SPY?

Two reasons: (1) The algorithm's edge has been validated specifically on QQQ's price history — the Nasdaq-100 has distinct momentum and drawdown characteristics that the rules are tuned for. (2) Concentration in tech-heavy QQQ amplifies the value of a regime filter — periods like 2000-2002 and 2022 saw catastrophic drawdowns that a rotation strategy can mitigate.

Will Ocean Blue ever cover other tickers?

Possibly in the future. Adding a new ticker requires the same rigorous research and 6-month live paper trading validation. Not something to expect in year one.

What if the strategy stops working?

It might. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. The algorithm's rules are based on patterns observed over 22 years of market history — if market structure changes fundamentally, those patterns may break. Subscribers are responsible for monitoring their own results and deciding whether the strategy continues to meet their goals. Cancel anytime.

What if Ocean Blue goes out of business?

If for any reason Ocean Blue stops operating, all subscribers will receive 30 days' notice and any unused subscription fees prorated to the cancellation date. Your positions in QQQ/TLT are held in your own IRA account — Ocean Blue never touches your money.

How is my email/SMS protected?

Email and phone numbers are stored for the sole purpose of delivering signals. We never sell or share contact information. Unsubscribe at any time removes you completely. See the privacy policy for full details.

Can I see the source code?

No. The signal-generating algorithm is the proprietary intellectual property of Best Kind LLC. The execution platform (paper trading display, web infrastructure) is separate and may be open-sourced in the future.

Who runs Ocean Blue?

Ocean Blue is a service of Best Kind LLC, a Brooklyn-based independent inventor working across multiple technology and finance projects. See the about page for more.

Is this gambling? Day-trading? Speculation?

No. Ocean Blue is a tactical rotation strategy that holds positions for weeks to months on average, with ~25 position changes per year. This is roughly the trading frequency of a managed futures fund or trend-following hedge fund — not day-trading.
